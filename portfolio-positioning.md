# Portfolio positioning research

Reviewed November 18, 2026. This is a qualitative comparison of role descriptions with the existing portfolio, not a statistical survey of hiring demand. Job examples illustrate requirements and terminology; they are not a shortlist of local vacancies, and availability may change.

## Recommendation

Use **Data Analyst** as the primary role, with **AI & Machine Learning** visible alongside Python and SQL. Emphasize data quality, statistical reasoning, operational problem solving, and clear communication. Describe responsible AI and algorithm auditing as a career direction in the summary. Invite opportunities across analytics, data quality, and applied AI in private and public organizations.

“Data Professional” leaves the function unspecified. “Algorithm Auditor” would currently promise work that the featured projects do not demonstrate: evaluating models, documenting risks, and assessing outcomes. This is an assessment of the portfolio's evidence, not a limit on possible career paths.

## What the sources show

- [O*NET: Business Intelligence Analysts](https://www.onetonline.org/link/details/15-2051.01) describes querying repositories, identifying patterns, and reporting findings. Those activities align with the portfolio's analytical work and communication background.
- [Kpler: Junior Data Quality Analyst](https://jobs.lever.co/kpler/caa9dbc6-f48f-437b-994f-64d233010da7) combines SQL, Python familiarity, anomaly investigation, root-cause analysis, and stakeholder communication. It also asks for BI tooling and data-focused experience. This supports data quality as an adjacent target while identifying skills and evidence to strengthen. The example is in London.
- [RTX: AI Governance Analyst](https://globalhr.wd5.myworkdayjobs.com/REC_RTX_Ext_Gateway/job/US-NY-REMOTE/AI-Governance-Analyst_01859101) includes control testing, impact assessments, model documentation, incident investigation, and cross-functional reporting. Its published requirements include eight years of relevant experience with a university degree, or five with an advanced degree. An “analyst” title alone does not imply an entry-level opening.
- [NIST: AI RMF Core, Measure function](https://airc.nist.gov/airmf-resources/airmf/5-sec-core/) describes documented evaluation of validity, reliability, explainability, fairness, and bias. These are useful targets for an audit-oriented portfolio project. Studying the framework does not establish audit experience or certification.

## Evidence to emphasize now

- Instacart: missing-value handling, duplicate removal, table joins, purchasing patterns, and visual explanation.
- Megaline: simulated customer data, revenue aggregation, hypothesis testing, and business recommendations.
- Video games: data preparation, selection of a relevant historical window, regional comparisons, and hypothesis testing. The page describes exploratory analysis rather than a validated predictive forecasting model.
- Amazon: discrepancy investigation, workflow exceptions, process changes, and associate support.
- Journalism and content strategy: accuracy, research, editing, and explaining findings to different audiences.

Course projects should be identified as projects, with recommendations distinguished from measured business results. SQL is listed in the training, but none of the three featured summaries currently demonstrates SQL work. Machine learning belongs in the training description until a featured project provides concrete evidence.

## Additional curriculum evidence

The supplied program screenshot broadens the technical foundation beyond the three published projects. It shows completed coursework through Sprint 15: Python, exploratory and statistical analysis, software development tools, the video game project, SQL, introductory ML, feature engineering, APIs, Docker, deep learning, AWS for ML, computer vision, and NLP. LLMs and generative AI, MLOps and production systems, and the capstone are listed as current or upcoming work. Coursework completion supports describing these areas as training; it does not establish production experience or completion of the whole certificate.

The revised positioning connects analytics to real-world validation: whether data represents relevant populations and conditions, whether metrics measure the intended concept, and whether results remain useful in practice. Feedback from users and affected people belongs alongside technical evaluation. This gives the responsible AI direction a concrete purpose while keeping the public copy constructive and broadly relevant to employers.

## Practical next steps

1. Add a SQL project with joins, validation checks, an issue log, and a short findings report. A data-quality dashboard would also address a gap illustrated by the Kpler role.
2. Build one model evaluation case study with a baseline, held-out evaluation, leakage checks, error analysis across relevant groups, and documented limitations. Define the real-world outcome, examine whether its label or proxy actually measures it, and identify whose experience the dataset may miss. Include a plan to compare results with operational evidence and stakeholder feedback; distinguish feedback actually gathered from proposed validation. Explain the use case and harms before selecting fairness metrics. Present it as a learning project.
3. Publish the evaluation notebook alongside a model card and a brief report of findings, unresolved risks, and recommended follow-up. Map relevant evidence to NIST's Measure function without claiming certification or regulatory compliance.

Search initially for Data Analyst, Junior Data Analyst, Data Quality Analyst, and operations analytics roles. Also explore AI Governance Analyst, Responsible AI Analyst, AI Quality Analyst, and AI Assurance roles, screening each description for experience requirements. Use the audit case study to strengthen applications for support work on those teams.

## Website updates

The homepage leads with reliable data and responsible decisions and explains the responsible AI direction in the summary. Skills distinguish applied analytics from the broader AI/ML coursework. Education separates completed coursework from current and upcoming modules and explicitly identifies the program as in progress. Project summaries emphasize methods and proposed recommendations. The contact page uses matching branding and invites relevant opportunities. No model audits, credentials, employment, or business outcomes have been invented.
